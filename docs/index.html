Malaysian new-property facts, inside your AI.

For property agents. Your AI answers with current prices, offers and rules for new projects, each one dated and with its source. Works in Claude and ChatGPT.

Install

claude plugin marketplace add Daryl0101/property-kb-plugin && claude plugin install property-kb@property-kb

Run it in a terminal where Claude Code is installed. It adds the tools and the skills together.

Install the Property KB plugin for me. Read https://property-kb-plugin-64268236583.us-central1.run.app/install and follow the steps for the app you are running in, one at a time.

Paste it into Claude, ChatGPT or Codex. Your AI walks you through the setup for the app you are in. To add it by hand, the connector address is https://property-kb-plugin-64268236583.us-central1.run.app/mcp.

After installing

  1. Sign in with GoogleYour AI opens the sign-in page the first time. Give your phone number once.
  2. Wait for approvalProperty KB is for property agents. We check and approve new accounts, and may call you first.
  3. SubscribeOnce approved, your AI gives you a link to pay by card on Stripe. Then ask your question again.
