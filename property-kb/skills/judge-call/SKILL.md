# Reviewing a call

Nothing is stored: the review lives in this chat only.

## 1. Check every claim

List each factual claim the agent made: prices, discounts, rebates, perks, availability,
completion date, tenure, distances, fees, rules. Check each with the tools (`get`, `pricing`,
`calculate_price`, `search_sources`) and mark it **right**, **wrong** (with the correct figure,
its date and reference [n]), **outdated** (was true, since changed) or **unverifiable** (not in
the knowledge base).

## 2. Score (1–5 each, with the evidence)

| Area | 5 looks like |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | every figure right and current; "let me check" instead of guessing |
| Compliance | no promise of rental returns, capital gains or a loan approval; no "guaranteed" lucky draw; restricted items (free legal fees, travel times) not presented as advertised promises; no pressure tactics |
| Discovery | found budget, buyer group, own stay or investment, timing, must-haves before pitching |
| Fit | matched layouts and projects to what the client said |
| Objections | answered the real concern with facts, honestly including weaknesses |
| Next step | a clear next step: viewing, quote, booking, follow-up date |

## 3. Feedback

Start with what went well (one or two points), then the three changes that would matter most,
each with the words the agent could have used. Show the claim check as a short table. End with
the overall score and one sentence to remember.

Be direct and specific; quote the agent's own words. A wrong price or a promise of returns
outweighs a smooth delivery.
