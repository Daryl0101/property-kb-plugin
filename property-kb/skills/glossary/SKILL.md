# Malaysian property glossary

Generated from Property KB's own glossary (`pkb plugin glossary`); do not edit by hand.
Figures a term mentions (thresholds, rates) are examples: current values come from the
tools or the current rules, never from here.

## Regulators, laws and permits

- **KPKT / JPN**: Ministry of Housing and Local Government / National Housing Department: licenses developers and approves ads.
- **HDA (Act 118)**: Housing Development (Control and Licensing) Act 1966: prescribed SPA, HDA account, defect liability.
- **APDL**: Advertising Permit and Developer's Licence. Ads and sales may only start once issued; ads must show its numbers.
- **No Lesen / No Permit**: Lesen Pemaju gives the developer's licence number; Permit Iklan dan Jualan gives the advertising permit number. Two different numbers.
- **Kod Pemaju**: The developer's KPKT code, printed on the permit. Not the licence number.
- **Alamat Berdaftar**: The developer's registered address on a permit or licence; not the site.
- **HDA account**: Housing Development Account: buyers' payments go here, withdrawals controlled by KPKT.
- **Strata Titles Act 1985 (Act 318)**: Governs strata titles for high-rise and gated units.
- **SiFUS**: Sijil Formula Unit Syer (Certificate of Share Unit Formula), from the state land office (PTG); required before strata units are sold.
- **Schedule of Parcels (Jadual Petak)**: Parcel list filed with the Commissioner of Buildings before strata sales.
- **COB**: Commissioner of Buildings: oversees strata management.
- **SMA 2013**: Strata Management Act 2013: maintenance charges, sinking fund, JMB/MC.
- **JMB / MC**: Joint Management Body (before strata titles are issued) / Management Corporation (after).
- **BOVAEA / LPPEH (Act 242)**: Board regulating valuers and estate agents.
- **REA / PEA / REN**: Registered Estate Agent / Probationary Estate Agent / Real Estate Negotiator (works under an REA); each has a registration number.
- **Tribunal for Homebuyer Claims**: Low-cost tribunal for buyer–developer disputes.
- **BNM**: Bank Negara Malaysia: sets loan rules (LTV, net selling price).

## Land, title and tenure

- **Freehold / leasehold**: Owned indefinitely / for a fixed lease (e.g. 99 years, "valid till 4 March 2123").
- **Strata / individual / master title**: Title per parcel in a building / per landed lot / one title for the whole land before subdivision.
- **Residential vs commercial title**: Land use on the title. Commercial means commercial utility and assessment rates (serviced apartments, SoHo).
- **Malay Reserve Land**: Land that only Malays may own.
- **Bumi lot / bumi quota**: Units reserved for Bumiputera buyers (at least 30% by common practice; set by each state).
- **Bumi discount**: State-set discount for Bumiputera buyers (e.g. about 5% KL, about 7% Selangor).
- **Bumi release**: State approval to sell an unsold bumi lot to non-Bumi, usually with a penalty.
- **Lot / PT no.**: Land lot number; "PT" is a provisional lot before final survey.
- **Mukim**: Land sub-district.
- **Assessment (cukai taksiran) / quit rent (cukai tanah)**: Annual local-council / land tax.

## Property and unit types

- **Condominium**: Residential-titled strata high-rise with facilities.
- **Serviced apartment (Pangsapuri Servis / Suite)**: Usually commercial-titled strata residence.
- **SoHo / SoFo / SoVo**: Small Office Home Office (can be lived in, HDA-protected) / Flexible Office / Versatile Office (office use, not HDA).
- **Terrace (link), semi-D, bungalow, townhouse, shop office**: Landed types.
- **Dual key**: One unit split into two self-contained parts.
- **Duplex / penthouse / loft / studio**: Unit forms.
- **"2+1"**: 2 bedrooms plus a study or utility room.
- **Built-up**: Internal floor area of a unit (sq ft).
- **Tandem parking**: Two bays one behind the other.
- **Facing / view**: Unit orientation / outlook (KLCC view, park view).

## Buying process and documents

- **EOI / registration of interest**: Pre-launch sign-up, before booking.
- **Booking fee**: Deposit to reserve a unit; counts toward the 10% down payment.
- **Booking form / OTP (Offer to Purchase)**: Form signed with the booking fee.
- **SPA**: Sale and Purchase Agreement, statutory form: Schedule G (landed) / Schedule H (strata).
- **SPA price**: Price in the SPA.
- **Down payment**: Usually 10% on signing the SPA.
- **Progressive billing**: Payments due at construction stages, per the SPA's payment schedule (Third Schedule).
- **Stakeholder sum**: Part of the price held by the developer's lawyer until defects are fixed.
- **Letter of Offer (LO)**: Bank's loan offer.
- **Loan agreement**: Loan contract; stamp duty applies.
- **MOT**: Memorandum of Transfer (title transfer); stamp duty applies.
- **VP**: Vacant possession: keys handed over.
- **CCC (replaced CF in 2007)**: Certificate of Completion and Compliance: building fit to occupy.
- **DLP**: Defect Liability Period, 24 months from VP under Schedules G/H.
- **LAD**: Liquidated Ascertained Damages for late delivery, 10% a year of the SPA price, counted daily.
- **EOT**: Extension of time granted to the developer; changes the completion date.
- **Sell-then-build / build-then-sell (10:90)**: Buyer pays progressively during construction / 10% now, 90% on completion.
- **Key handover**: VP ceremony.
- **Subsale vs primary / new launch**: Resale from an owner vs first sale from a developer.

## Price, promotions and financing

- **Referral fee / repeat buyer reward**: Paid to a buyer who refers another buyer or buys again; not an agent's commission.
- **Nett price / net selling price**: Price after all rebates, discounts and incentives. Since Nov 2013 BNM bases LTV on it.
- **Discount**: Lowers the SPA price.
- **Rebate**: SPA price unchanged; money given back or offset.
- **Cashback**: Cash paid to the buyer after purchase.
- **"From RM 5xxk"**: Approximate starting price, digits hidden.
- **psf**: Price per sq ft of built-up.
- **Early bird / VVIP preview / balloting**: Launch-phase deals and unit selection.
- **Free legal fees (SPA / loan)**: Developer pays the buyer's legal fees.
- **Stamp duty waiver / first-home exemption**: Developer pays it, or a government exemption (e.g. full MOT and loan stamp-duty exemption for a first home up to RM 500k).
- **Furnishing package**: Fittings or furniture bundled in.
- **5:95 / 10:90 / zero down payment**: Deferred payment schemes.
- **DIBS**: Developer Interest Bearing Scheme (developer pays loan interest during construction); banned by BNM for new financing since Nov 2013.
- **Guaranteed rental return (GRR)**: Developer promises rental income for a period.
- **LTV / margin of finance**: Loan as a % of price: usually up to 90% for the 1st and 2nd home, 70% from the 3rd.
- **Panel bank / end financing**: Banks that pre-approved the project.
- **LPPSA**: Civil-servant housing loan board.
- **EPF withdrawal**: Using EPF savings for a down payment or instalments.
- **RPGT**: Real Property Gains Tax on sale.

## Ownership-cost terms

- **Maintenance fee / service charge**: Monthly strata charge, per sq ft.
- **Sinking fund**: Long-term repair reserve, at least 10% of the maintenance charge (SMA 2013 s.30(4)).
- **Commercial utility rates**: Higher electricity and water rates for commercial-titled units.

## Buyer schemes and groups

- **Foreigner minimum price**: State threshold for foreign buyers (e.g. RM 1m KL; RM 2m Selangor zones 1–2).
- **MM2H**: Malaysia My Second Home visa programme.
- **PR1MA / RUMAWIP (Residensi Wilayah) / Rumah Selangorku**: Federal / Federal Territory / Selangor affordable-housing schemes with income caps and resale moratoriums.
- **My First Home Scheme / SJKP**: Government-backed loan-guarantee schemes (SJKP for self-employed and gig workers).
- **First-time buyer**: Buyer owning no home; eligible for stamp-duty exemptions.
